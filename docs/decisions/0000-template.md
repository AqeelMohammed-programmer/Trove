# 0000. ADR template

**Status:** Template
**Date:** —

## Context

What situation forces a decision? Include the constraints that matter (the
spec, the stack table in `AGENTS.md`, operational reality). State the problem,
not the solution.

## Decision

What was decided, stated in the active voice. Include the concrete choice (a
library name and version, a format, a command) so a reader can act on it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must be maintained because
of this decision. Include the costs and the follow-up work.

## Alternatives considered

The realistic options that were rejected, and why. One short paragraph each.
